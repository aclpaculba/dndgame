Ability Scores

Every stat matters in a fight, not just whichever one comes up in the story — here's what each one actually does.

Strength (STR)Raw power. Increases the damage you deal.
Dexterity (DEX)Speed and precision. Boosts both your damage and your chance to dodge incoming attacks.
Constitution (CON)Toughness. Reduces how much damage you take from every hit.
Intelligence (INT)Tactical insight. Helps you find and exploit an enemy's weak points for bonus damage.
Wisdom (WIS)Awareness. Helps you see danger coming, reducing damage taken.
Charisma (CHA)Leadership. Boosts the whole party's luck on your best moments, and softens the fallout of your worst.

Spending a stat point when you level up raises that ability permanently — the higher it gets, the more it helps. Even so, a lucky or unlucky roll can still swing a fight hard either way.
